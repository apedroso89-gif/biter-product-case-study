# Product Brief: Biter

## Vision

To redefine food discovery by connecting people with dietary needs to trusted, community-validated dining experiences, so they can choose where to eat with confidence.

## Problem statement

Users with dietary restrictions struggle to confidently choose restaurants because reliable dietary information is missing. This leads to time-consuming research and reduced willingness to dine out.

1. **Lack of trust:** menus often lack detailed allergen information, so users are unsure what's safe to eat.
2. **Time-consuming research:** users check multiple sources (reviews, social media) or call restaurants ahead.

## Customer segments

**Primary (early adopters: high motivation, clear pain)**
- Lactose-intolerant diners: need clear dairy-free options with verified ingredient information
- Gluten-sensitive diners: need cross-contamination awareness and gluten-free options

**Secondary (expansion)**
- Allergy-affected diners: nuts, shellfish and other allergies requiring near-absolute certainty
- Health-conscious diners: seeking nutritional transparency

**Shared needs:** trustworthy information · clear dietary labeling · confidence before choosing

## Persona: Sarah Mitchell

34, marketing manager in Portland, lactose intolerant.

> "I want to enjoy food without constantly worrying about my stomach. Finding good alternatives shouldn't be this hard."

- **Motivations:** keep her social life without restrictions holding her back; find alternatives that taste good; share experiences to help others
- **Pain points:** hidden dairy ingredients; limited menu options; social awkwardness of asking staff many questions
- **Behaviors:** researches restaurants before going out; uses Yelp and HappyCow; shares reviews on social media

## Value proposition

For people with dietary restrictions who struggle to trust restaurant options, **Biter** is a social food discovery platform that connects users with trusted local restaurants through community-validated insights, bringing confidence and peace of mind to every dining decision.

**Unlike** traditional platforms that rely on unstructured reviews, Biter provides **structured, dish-level, community-validated** information.

**Differentiators:** dish-level granularity · community validation · confidence before choosing · social discovery layer

## Hypothesis

Changing unreliable dietary information into community-validated restaurant insight will increase user confidence and improve conversion and retention among users with dietary needs.

## MVP prioritization (MoSCoW)

| Must have | Should have | Could have | Won't have (MVP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dietary filters (lactose-free, gluten-free) | More dietary categories | Confidence score | Full allergen database |
| Basic dish-level tagging | Improved tagging accuracy | Personalized recommendations | Advanced AI recommendations |
| "Was this safe?" Yes/No validation | | | |

## MVP success criteria

| Area | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Adoption | Users applying dietary filters | 50% |
| Engagement | Active users contributing at least one validation in month 1 | 30% |
| Trust | Surveyed users reporting increased dining confidence | 80% |

## OKRs

**O1: Improve confidence in restaurant selection**
- KR: Increase filter usage from 40% to 65%
- KR: Increase restaurant selection conversion from 30% to 50%

**O2: Increase engagement**
- KR: Increase saved restaurants per user from 2 to 2.3 (+15%)
- KR: Increase restaurant views per session by 10%

**O3: Build trust in community validation**
- KR: Reach a 30% feedback submission rate
- KR: Maintain a trust rating of at least 4/5
