# Research Plan: Dietary Dining Discovery

**Jira:** SCRUM-7 · **Epic:** Research & Discovery · **Owner:** Aline Pedroso (PM) · **Status:** Draft

## Background

People with dietary restrictions appear to spend a lot of effort deciding where it's safe to eat. Before designing a solution, we need to understand how they choose restaurants today, where trust breaks down, and what would give them confidence.

## Objectives

1. Understand how people with dietary restrictions currently find and choose restaurants
2. Identify where and why trust in dietary information breaks down
3. Learn which sources they trust most, and why
4. Identify which restrictions are the most underserved, to focus the MVP

## Research questions

- Which dietary restrictions should Biter focus on first?
- What information do people need before deciding a restaurant is suitable?
- What frustrates them most when searching for restaurants?
- How do they verify dietary information today?
- Whose recommendations do they trust, and what makes a source trustworthy?

## Assumptions to test

These are **assumptions, not findings.** The research should confirm or reject each one.

| # | Assumption | How we'll know |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Lack of trust in menu information is the main barrier, more than lack of options | Participants describe uncertainty, not scarcity, as their main frustration |
| A2 | People spend significant time researching before dining out | Participants describe multi-step research (several sources, calling ahead) |
| A3 | Recommendations from people with the same restriction are the most trusted source | Participants rank peer experience above reviews and restaurant claims |
| A4 | Dish-level information is more useful than restaurant-level ratings | Participants talk about specific dishes, not just "allergy-friendly" restaurants |

## Method

- **Semi-structured interviews**, 30–40 minutes, remote (video call)
- **Competitive analysis** of Yelp, HappyCow and Google Reviews (SCRUM-10)

## Participants

**Target:** 8–10 participants

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Dietary restriction | Lactose intolerance, gluten sensitivity/celiac, or food allergy (aim for a mix) |
| Age | 25–45 |
| Dining habits | Eats out at least 2 times a week |
| Location | Lives in an urban area |

**Recruiting channels:** dietary-restriction Facebook groups and subreddits, personal network, LinkedIn post.
